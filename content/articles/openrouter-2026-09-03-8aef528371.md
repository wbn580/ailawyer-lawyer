## How it works

We built an open-source OpenRouter that uses your usage to improve the model. The system routes your requests and collects usage data from those requests. This data is then used to improve the model, making it better over time.

## What makes it open

The entire project is open source, so you can inspect and modify the code. It's a transparent alternative to closed routers.

## What you get

- Better model performance as usage data accumulates
- Full control over your routing and data
- Ability to self-host and customize
