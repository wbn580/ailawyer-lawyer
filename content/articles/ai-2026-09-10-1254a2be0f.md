## What the National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare has issued

The National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare has issued recommendations on a future regulatory framework for AI in healthcare. The recommendations are published under the title "National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare: Recommendations for a future regulatory framework" on GOV.UK, and the same title is also published as a PDF file of 4.62 MB.

The body is named the National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare. Its recommendations are the subject of this article.

## Why the Commission was established

The National Commission into the Regulation of AI in Healthcare was established to advise government on a future regulatory framework for AI in healthcare. That advisory purpose defines the scope of the work: the Commission's output is advice to government on a future framework, and the recommendations published on GOV.UK are that advice.

## What the Commission's stated mission covers

The Commission states its mission in three parts.

![Illustration of healthcare AI regulation documents on a desk](https://img.ailawyer.lawyer/AI工具评测-法律/ai-2026-09-10-1254a2be0f-image-02-2026-1880x1253.jpg)


- First, the mission is to help ensure that patients can benefit from safe and effective AI technologies.
- Second, the mission is to support the government's ambition to make the NHS the most AI-enabled healthcare system in the world.
- Third, the mission is to establish a globally competitive regulatory environment that attracts investment and supports innovation.

Each of these elements is part of the mission as the Commission expresses it. They sit alongside the recommendations for a future regulatory framework rather than replacing them.
