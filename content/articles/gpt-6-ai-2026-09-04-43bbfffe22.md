## What Makes GPT-6 Different?

GPT-6 is designed to both handle tasks and maintain oversight. According to the announcement, it combines advanced capability with integrated safety features.

## How Does GPT-6 Handle Safety?

The model includes safety measures that allow it to operate while remaining under control. These features are built into the system, not added as an afterthought.

## Why This Balance Matters

For legal professionals, an AI that can work and be watched is more useful than one that excels at tasks but lacks oversight. The focus on balancing capability with safety addresses a common concern.
