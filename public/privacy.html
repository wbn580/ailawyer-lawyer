Privacy

We don't ask for personal details to answer you; if you choose to leave a contact for follow-up, it's used only to arrange that. AILawyer is built to help you, not to gather data about you.

No accounts, passive follow-up only

There is no sign-up and no login. The assistant will not proactively demand your name, email, phone number or other contact details, and there are no forms designed to capture them. When you use the chat, the assistant may invite you to leave a contact (optional) if it seems a real person could follow up on your specific situation — you can always decline. If you choose to provide one, it is forwarded only to ben@arrivau.com and used solely to arrange that follow-up; it is not shared, sold, or used for marketing. If you happen to type personal details into a question without meaning to leave them for follow-up, those are not stored for marketing and are not shared.

Your conversation

Your messages are used only to answer you within the same session. We do not build a profile of you, track you across sites, or sell data. We keep, at most, anonymous aggregate counts (for example, how many questions were asked) that contain no personal information.

Third parties

To generate answers, your question text is processed by a language-model provider solely to produce a reply. We don't attach your identity to it.

Cookies

We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
